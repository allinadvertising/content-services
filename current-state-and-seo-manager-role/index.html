SEO Strategy Playbook

Initial setup builds the account strategy. Each month after that, the SEO Strategist pulls from a credit pool and recommends the work expected to move the needle most.

AM Gathers Context

Collect goals, priorities, access, client constraints, recent feedback, and business context before strategy work begins.

Strategist Sets Priorities

Translate that context into the SEO tasks most likely to support the client's goals and make the best use of available credits.

AM Aligns the Client

Discuss the recommended plan with the client, confirm buy-in, and bring new client direction back into the strategy loop.

Initial Setup

Map priority pages, review current rankings, identify gaps, define commercial keyword groups, and set the first-month action plan.

Priority Products, Collections, and Commercial Pages

Identify the highest-value Shopify collections, products, and buying guides based on revenue potential, search demand, ranking opportunity, and inventory depth.

Money Keywords

Define transactional keywords tied to purchases and group them by collection, carat, shape, clarity, certification, price intent, and brand intent.

Current Top-Performing Keywords

Review keywords already driving clicks, impressions, and revenue, then improve CTR, collection content, internal links, and conversion paths.

Missing Keywords

Identify competitor keyword gaps and decide where new collection pages, buying guides, or educational content are needed.

Keywords Close to Page 1

Focus on terms ranking in positions 10 to 16 and prioritize quick-win optimizations for pages with strong commercial value.

CMS SEO Controls

Decide which collections should be indexable, control duplicate URLs, and strengthen links between blogs, guides, collections, and products.

Monthly Credit Allocation

Credits may go to different work each month. The Strategist chooses the best mix based on opportunity, urgency, and expected impact.

Operating principle: the monthly plan is not fixed. The SEO Strategist decides where credits create the best return based on the account's current opportunities.
