# Current State and SEO Manager Role

Date: 06-09-2026

Short presentation to showcase how to streamline SEO services.

## Current State

- 105 active SEO clients
- Nick should eventually focus on account management rather than SEO strategy

## SEO Manager Role

- Support all Account Managers with SEO strategy and implementation monitoring
- Free up Account Managers to focus fully on account management and handle more accounts
- Strategy development, execution review, and follow-up would sit with this role
- You can cover the next 1 to 1.5 months by managing 20 to 30 accounts, with a focus on at-risk accounts and new accounts
- The goal during that period is to document SOPs for onboarding, recovery plans for red-code accounts, and ongoing services
- Ricky is an SEO Specialist focused on implementation, not strategy, so he is not the right fit for this role
- You are already delegating technical tasks to developers, which means your current responsibilities are 100% strategy-focused

## SEO Manager Capacity

- Before AI: 10 to 20 accounts
- Today, with AI, strong SOPs, and skills integrated into an agent: 40 to 50 accounts, each with a quarterly strategy

## What Managing Multiple Accounts Involves

- The initial setup takes most of the time
- Once the strategy is in motion, implementation can be distributed across Ricky for SEO, Heidi for content, and developers for technical SEO
- The main workload is in the setup phase, not in ongoing maintenance

## Quarterly Strategy as the Priority

- The strategy should be defined within the first 2 to 3 days after onboarding
- Immediate access to GSC and Google Analytics is required
- The strategy must be based on the site's actual performance, not assumptions
