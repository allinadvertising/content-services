Why Content Services

The Market Has Shifted

Technical SEO creates the foundation: crawlable, indexable sites. But in the AI overview era, content depth and authority drive visibility. Clients who stop at technical SEO hit a ranking ceiling.

The Agency Wins

Technical SEO + Content is a stickier, higher-value retainer. Few agencies combine deep technical with strategic content under one roof: that is our differentiator.

Expected Outcomes

  • Higher client retention and LTV
  • Larger monthly retainers per account
  • Measurable ranking and traffic growth
  • Clear competitive differentiation
  • Natural upsell path from technical SEO
  • Recurring revenue stream beyond one-time audits
3 / 15

Why Content. Why Now.

Why Upsell Clients on Content

  • Own the full SEO strategy: technical foundation plus page-level content.
  • Link building has lost effectiveness in the AI overview era; crawlability, indexability, discoverability, and useful content matter more.
  • We already solve indexation issues, crawl budget waste, and discovery blockers.
  • Audits keep finding the same wall: outdated blogs, thin product/collection pages, or no copy.
  • Collection-page descriptions have already helped clients rank better for top-priority keywords.

How This Fits Growth

  • Prioritize transactional-intent keywords over informational queries absorbed by AI overviews.
  • Show buyers the value of technical and high-ticket products, not just get pages indexed.
  • Deliver content that converts: value props, specs, use cases, fitment, and buying confidence.

Core idea: technical SEO opens the door; content controls what users and search engines find once they get there.

Service Scope: What We Produce

1. Keyword Research and Strategy

Define transactional targets, intent clusters, priority pages, and the new vs. existing content plan.

2. Content Audit and Optimization

Find thin, outdated, duplicate, or missing copy and prioritize product, collection, and money-page updates.

3. Content Calendar

3-month production schedule for new pages, content hubs, and existing URL updates.

4. New Content Production

Brief > AM approval > draft > client approval > publish-ready copy. We write the content, not only the brief.

5. Existing Content Updates

Fix H1/H2/H3 hierarchy, align headings with search intent, and add at least 300 useful words per selected URL.

AI Search and Merchant Center Fit

AI Search Strategy

  • No major strategy change: agents still need useful, crawlable, indexable sources.
  • Prioritize transactional pages that help users choose products and services.
  • Make product value, specs, fitment, use cases, and buying confidence explicit.
  • New SEO is optimization for search engines and agent-assisted searches.

Merchant Center Split

  • Technical SEO owns robots.txt, crawl access, feed access, and discovery blockers.
  • Content services owns product and category description enhancements.
  • AI can update Merchant Center-relevant product descriptions in bulk.
  • Content side handles keyword-relevant, user-facing copy when description quality is the gap.

Simple split: technical access stays with Technical SEO; product and category copy improvements live in Content Services.

Position Tracking as Early Proof

  • Build a position tracking project for each client around top-priority keywords.
  • Segment keywords by service, location, intent, and content cluster.
  • Use Ahrefs tracking capacity to monitor up to 5,000 total keywords across active clients.
  • Report movement into top 20, top 10, and top 3 positions as leading indicators.

Revenue Attribution as Long-Term Proof

  • Track organic entrances, assisted conversions, leads, and booked revenue by content URL.
  • Compare new content and refreshed pages against pre-launch baselines.
  • After 6+ months, begin showing revenue increases connected to content creation.
  • Use revenue lift to justify retainer expansion and ongoing content investment.

Client-facing narrative: first we prove visibility is improving, then we prove that visibility is turning into qualified traffic, leads, and revenue.
