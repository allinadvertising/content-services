Why Content Services Now

The Market Has Shifted

Technical SEO creates the foundation: crawlable, indexable sites. But in the AI overview era, content depth and authority drive visibility. Clients who stop at technical SEO hit a ranking ceiling.

The Agency Wins

Technical SEO + Content is a stickier, higher-value retainer. Few agencies combine deep technical with strategic content under one roof: that is our differentiator.

Expected Outcomes

  • Higher client retention and LTV
  • Larger monthly retainers per account
  • Measurable ranking and traffic growth
  • Clear competitive differentiation
  • Natural upsell path from technical SEO
  • Recurring revenue stream beyond one-time audits

Service Scope: What We Deliver

1. Keyword Research and Strategy

Full keyword universe, intent clustering, priority scoring, competitor gap analysis. Deliverable: strategy document with prioritized targets.

2. Content Audit and Optimization

Crawl and score existing content. Flag thin, outdated, or duplicate pages. Identify quick wins in positions 4-15. Deliverable: audit report with action plan.

3. Content Calendar

3-month rolling editorial calendar aligned with keyword priorities. Topic clusters mapped to pillar pages. Deliverable: editable calendar.

4. Content Briefs and Guidelines

Detailed briefs per piece: target keyword, intent, structure, word count, internal links. AI-assisted drafting for speed. Deliverable: briefs in batches.

5. Performance Tracking

Monthly ranking, traffic, and conversion reports. Content refresh recommendations. Ongoing keyword discovery. Deliverable: dashboard + optimization notes.

Go / No-Go: Decision Framework

GO Criteria

  • 2-3 current technical SEO clients show interest in content services
  • 60-hour monthly capacity is protected from technical SEO overflow
  • Leadership agrees on pricing model and margin targets
  • Content specialist hire approved for Phase 2 if Wave 1 succeeds

NO-GO Criteria

  • No clear client demand signal exists
  • Technical SEO workload cannot accommodate the 60-hour reallocation
  • Pricing does not meet margin requirements
  • Leadership prefers to stay focused on technical SEO exclusively
